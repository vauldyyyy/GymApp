# FORMA design direction

Working name: FORMA. “Made for your momentum.” The name has not undergone trademark or app-store availability clearance.

An editorial training companion: warm ivory surfaces, charcoal text, sage accents, and energetic orange. Strength without intimidation. Photography shows a focused moment rather than a transformation promise.

The UI/UX Pro Max fitness training mobile search matched a fitness product, orange energy, strong type, clear blocks and purposeful motion. The earlier premium query skewed toward fashion/glass; that result was not adopted. Final choices use Manrope for expressive headings, DM Sans for readable utility, restrained transitions and reduced-motion support. Lucide provides a consistent vector icon family.

## Product hierarchy

1. Today: the next session and a truthful weekly overview.
2. My plan: selected training days with explicit recovery days.
3. Explore: adapted sessions, searchable exercise guidance, saved favorites.
4. Progress: completed work, recorded weights and actual history.
5. Profile: training preferences, account, sync, units and membership.

Onboarding: value → goal → experience → equipment → days → time/name → concrete first week. A live preview uses the actual plan builder: visible beside questions on desktop and available through “Preview my plan” on phones. Short answer feedback explains changes, and the final week offers direct edits to goal, equipment, and schedule. The primary action stays available in portrait; short landscape screens use an in-scroll layout. Name is optional; account and health permissions are not prerequisites. The plan is deterministic and preference-based, not represented as AI or medically individualized advice.

Onboarding motion establishes continuity: an original three-bar FORMA emblem settles inside fine rings, questions slide and fade in the direction of navigation, and selection checks and icons use a small spring. The emblem entrance is finite and bounded at 1.3 seconds. Its five dots reflect setup progress, not health or readiness. Reduced motion displays settled states without decorative movement. The BodBot recording informed the live preview and transition approach; the visual identity and assets remain FORMA's. See [onboarding-reference.md](onboarding-reference.md) for the observed reference and adaptation.

## Functional scope

Seven starting workout templates and 22 exercise guides. Plans adapt exercises to available equipment, time, goal and experience. Logging includes per-set weights/reps, prior performance, rest timing, exercise swaps, completion and durable resume.

Core training stays free. The current Plus benefit is unlimited workout bookmarks; free profiles can save three. Billing prices/periods come only from real store packages. No fake checkout or fabricated trial eligibility.
